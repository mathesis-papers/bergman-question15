# Examples answering Bergman's Question 15 by extending a partition of von zur Gathen and Roche

**Authors:** Haobo Ma, Wenlin Zhang

We answer Question 15 of George M. Bergman's Thoughts on Eggert's Conjecture, arXiv:1206.0326v2, Section 4, printed page 6, in the negative, including over Q by extending a binomial partition of von zur Gathen and Roche (1997, Table 2, p. 359) to a semigroup. A 59-element commutative semigroup and a 49-element quotient have three generators with distinct nonzero sixth powers but a nonzero linear combination whose sixth power is zero. For these presentations over a characteristic-zero field, the cancellation exists exactly when the field contains a primitive cube root of unity. The construction extends to every positive exponent divisible by six. The older partition yields a 632-element two-generator example at exponent 35 over every characteristic-zero field.
